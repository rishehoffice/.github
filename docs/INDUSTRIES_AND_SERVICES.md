## How to navigate this atlas

Find your business context below. Each section explains **the friction**, **the solution shape**, **the RISHEH services involved**, and **a linked example when one is documented**.

### Education & Schools

**Business friction:** People, classes, academic data, parent communication and school administration can become disconnected.

**Solution shape:** Multi-role school platform; school-specific experiences on shared foundations; controlled access; educational and operational workflows.

**RISHEH services:** Product discovery · Custom web applications · UX/UI · Identity and roles · Workflow integration · Data/analytics design.

**Featured documented work:** [Hamyar School case study](../case-studies/hamyar-school.md).

### Health & Specialized Services

**Business friction:** Visitors need trusted content, understandable services, specialist context and an accessible path to consultation.

**Solution shape:** Content-led discovery with service navigation, verified professional content and structured consultation intake. Privacy and careful handling of health-related information matter.

**RISHEH services:** Service website/PWA · RTL UX · Content architecture · Forms/intake · CRM handoff planning · SEO foundations.

**Featured documented work:** [Entekhab Platform case study](../case-studies/entekhab-platform.md).

### Commerce & Retail

**Business friction:** Catalogs, payments, orders, inventory, fulfillment and customer support create fragile handoffs when disconnected.

**Solution shape:** Storefront and commerce backend linked through explicit order states, integrations, reliability controls and useful operational views.

**RISHEH services:** E-commerce systems · Backend/API development · Payments/fulfillment architecture · Data flows · Automation · Conversion UX.

**Featured public engineering work:** [RISHEH Pay case study](../case-studies/risheh-pay.md) and [public source](https://github.com/mahamirmh/risheh-pay).

### Professional, Legal & Advisory Services

**Business friction:** Expertise and trust can be hard to communicate; intake often arrives through disconnected channels.

**Solution shape:** Clear service architecture, editorial proof, role-appropriate intake, consultation flows and operational follow-up.

**RISHEH services:** Business websites · Service UX · SEO/content architecture · Intake forms · CRM workflow planning · Scheduling integrations.

**Evidence category:** Service capability / solution blueprint. No unverified client performance numbers are presented as a case study.

### Real Estate, Construction & Investment

**Business friction:** Buyers and investors need coherent project discovery, structured property/unit information, and clear consultation pathways.

**Solution shape:** Project and unit catalogs, detailed property content, lead and consultation journeys, and role-specific operational interfaces.

**RISHEH services:** Web applications · Information architecture · Lead capture · CRM integration · Search/filter UX · Visual storytelling.

**Evidence category:** Service capability / solution blueprint. Not presented as a completed client outcome without publishable evidence.

### Internal Operations & CRM

**Business friction:** Projects, tasks, documents, conversations and decisions fragment across tools.

**Solution shape:** Role-scoped internal workspaces, connected knowledge, execution workflows and operations dashboards.

**RISHEH services:** Internal platforms · Custom CRM/workspaces · System integration · Authorization design · Workflow automation · Data dashboards.

**Featured documented work:** [RISHEH Core case study](../case-studies/risheh-core.md).

### AI & Automation

**Business friction:** Scattered agent experiments and automations can lack clear authorization, observability and reusable interfaces.

**Solution shape:** Agent integration boundaries, safe automation, explicit execution states, structured retrieval and human oversight where actions are consequential.

**RISHEH services:** AI agents · Workflow automation · API/MCP integrations · Systems architecture · Governance patterns · Evaluation and auditing design.

**Featured documented work:** [RISHEH AI Platform](../case-studies/risheh-ai-platform.md) and [NHS JobBot](../case-studies/nhs-jobbot.md). [Public AI skills work](https://github.com/mahamirmh/risheh-claude-skills) illustrates multi-assistant workflow enablement.

### Career & Professional Platforms

**Business friction:** Career discovery, role matching, readiness and application follow-up are often manual and disjointed.

**Solution shape:** Normalized job information, inspectable matching policies, user preferences, alerts and assistance workflows.

**RISHEH services:** Product engineering · Data ingestion · Automation · AI-assisted experiences · Role-based interfaces.

**Featured documented work:** [NHS JobBot case study](../case-studies/nhs-jobbot.md).

### Web Experience & Product Design

**Business friction:** Generic templates ignore industry-specific buying journeys and make safe changes difficult as sites evolve.

**Solution shape:** Business-aware design discovery, visual systems, validated specifications and maintainable implementations.

**RISHEH services:** Product strategy · UX/UI · Design systems · Website engineering · SEO foundations · Performance/accessibility design.

**Featured public engineering work:** [Website Template Factory case study](../case-studies/website-template-factory.md) and [public source](https://github.com/mahamirmh/Risheh-Website-Template-Factory).

## Our delivery model

| Stage | What RISHEH is responsible for | Evidence to look for |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Discover** | Business goals, user journeys, workflows, constraints | Scope, requirements and priorities |
| **Design** | Product architecture, information structure, interaction decisions | UX flows, interface specifications |
| **Engineer** | Web systems, APIs, data boundaries and integrations | Working source, interfaces and tests |
| **Integrate** | Automation, operational handoffs and access rules | End-to-end workflow evidence |
| **Improve** | Measured observation, iteration and maintainability | Explicit metrics when available; no invented ROI |

**Editorial policy:** An internal product is not automatically a client project. A planned feature is not a shipped capability. A repository is not proof of a successful production release. Customer performance or endorsements require source evidence and publishing permission.
