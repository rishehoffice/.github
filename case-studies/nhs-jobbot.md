# 05 / NHS JobBot

**Career Technology** · Structured medical job discovery

[← All case studies](README.md) · [Publicly published project overview](https://github.com/mahamirmh/RISHEH-Industry-Solutions-Corporate-Profile#selected-work--five-different-systems)

## The business challenge

Doctors considering NHS roles often need to reconcile fragmented job sources, role titles, grades, sponsorship requirements, readiness documents, and application tracking. A generic keyword alert is not enough.

## Product and engineering direction

NHS JobBot is documented as a backend and Telegram-oriented product combining job ingestion, structured filtering, deterministic relevance matching, notifications, and application-assistance workflows.

## Services illustrated

- **Data ingestion and normalization:** Reconciling jobs from different source formats and avoiding duplicate records.
- **Rules-based matching:** Making job relevance easier to inspect rather than relying solely on opaque scores.
- **Automation:** Alerts, summaries and background processing.
- **AI-enabled assistance:** Support for writing and guidance within a broader structured workflow.

## Distinctive design decision

**Explain suitability; do not promise success.** A meaningful system exposes fit and blockers without representing a relevance score as hiring certainty.

## Evidence and current disclosure

The high-level product story appears in RISHEH's publicly available corporate portfolio. The engineering repository is private. This case does not disclose candidate profiles, vacancies tied to personal accounts, confidential integrations, or deployment secrets.

**Outcome we can responsibly state:** A documented structured career workflow and supporting engineering components. No guaranteed eligibility, interview, employment, or job-placement outcomes are claimed.
