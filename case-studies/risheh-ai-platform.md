# 04 / RISHEH AI Platform

**AI Infrastructure & Governance** · Shared integration boundaries

[← All case studies](README.md) · [Publicly published project overview](https://github.com/mahamirmh/RISHEH-Industry-Solutions-Corporate-Profile#selected-work--five-different-systems)

## The business challenge

AI features become difficult to maintain when each product independently reimplements tool access, execution state, integrations and permission decisions.

## Product and engineering direction

The RISHEH AI Platform initiative provides shared architectural contracts for AI-related capabilities while keeping application-specific business rules in their owning products. The documented direction separates reusable infrastructure from agent orchestration and domain logic.

## Services illustrated

- **AI systems architecture:** Reusable interfaces between products, agents, and tools.
- **Integration design:** Adapter-based boundaries instead of direct coupling to other products' databases.
- **Governance:** Auditable actions, typed inputs, and controlled execution decisions.
- **Engineering enablement:** Common contracts and reference implementations that reduce duplicated design work.

## Distinctive design decision

**Shared contracts; independent product ownership.** Centralization should reduce duplication without turning unrelated products into a single fragile runtime.

## Evidence and current disclosure

The public corporate profile describes the project's architectural purpose. Its source repository remains private, and the implementation distinguishes foundations/reference components from fully production-verified governance infrastructure.

**Outcome we can responsibly state:** An engineering initiative focused on reusable AI integration and governance patterns, not a claim that every planned control-plane component is deployed.
