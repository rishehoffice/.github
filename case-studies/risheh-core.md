## The business challenge

Modern project teams scatter decisions across chat, tasks, documents, engineering discussions and personal reminders. The result is less reliable institutional context and repeated coordination work.

## Product and engineering direction

RISHEH Core is an internal workspace initiative designed around connecting team communication, project execution, documentation, and engineering context without forcing people to duplicate the same decision in multiple tools.

## Services illustrated

- **Operational discovery:** Understanding team workflows and permission boundaries.
- **Internal product engineering:** Building connected project, task, discussion, and knowledge interfaces.
- **Workflow automation:** Supporting handoffs between decisions, engineering activity, and follow-up work.
- **Secure information architecture:** Separating sensitive records and scoped organizational information from broadly accessible collaboration surfaces.

## Distinctive design decision

**The unit of value is context, not another dashboard.** A task should preserve its relationship to the decision, conversation, and project that created it.

## Evidence and current disclosure

The public RISHEH corporate portfolio documents the product concept and scope. Its implementation repository is private. This public case avoids disclosing member information, internal data models, infrastructure details, or security-sensitive workflows.

**Outcome we can responsibly state:** A documented internal product initiative for connected operations. No invented adoption figures, productivity gains, or independently audited security claims.
