## The business challenge

Generic website templates often start with appearance rather than business purpose. Different industries need different trust signals, visitor journeys, content architecture and conversion actions.

## Product and engineering direction

RISHEH Website Template Factory documents a three-stage approach: **C1** composes business requirements into a validated specification; **C2** generates a Next.js project deterministically; **C3** compares changes and supports conservative regeneration that protects legitimate manual edits.

## Services illustrated

- **Industry discovery and UX strategy:** Business archetypes, intent-specific funnels, and sector-aware page composition.
- **Design systems:** Reusable visual languages and interaction patterns rather than copied websites.
- **Frontend engineering:** Structured generation of Next.js, React and TypeScript applications.
- **Developer tooling:** Validation, quality gates, provenance, and safe regeneration workflows.

## Distinctive design decision

**Regeneration must not silently overwrite human work.** The documented C3 mechanism classifies what may safely change and what requires a human decision.

## Evidence and current disclosure

The [public source repository](https://github.com/mahamirmh/Risheh-Website-Template-Factory) documents 16 industry families, business archetypes, design DNA profiles, build specifications, quality gates and the C1/C2/C3 architecture. These are repository capabilities, not claims about paying customers or deployed sites.

**Outcome we can responsibly state:** A publicly documented website generation system with explicit design/engineering boundaries and safety-conscious regeneration. No invented revenue, customer or conversion metrics.
