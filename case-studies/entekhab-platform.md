## The business challenge

For a specialized service organization, a visitor may first need reliable information, then help understanding available services, then a clear way to request consultation. A disconnected content site rarely supports this full decision journey.

## Product and engineering direction

Entekhab is documented as a Persian RTL responsive web product that connects service discovery, specialists, educational content, media, and consultation intake. The design focuses on moving from learning to a meaningful next step without using invented trust signals.

## Services illustrated

- **UX research and information architecture:** Structuring services, specialists, knowledge content, and the consultation journey.
- **Product design and frontend engineering:** Accessible, responsive RTL experiences and PWA-oriented behavior.
- **Content operations:** Editorial destinations for articles and educational media.
- **Lead intake and workflow design:** Consultation requests treated as an operational journey rather than a decorative contact button.

## Distinctive design decision

**Trust through provenance, not decoration.** A profile, image, rating, or claim must be grounded in actual source content; missing material should not be filled with fictional proof.
