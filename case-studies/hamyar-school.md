# 01 / Hamyar School

**Education & Schools** · Multi-tenant school operating system

[← All case studies](README.md) · [Publicly published project overview](https://github.com/mahamirmh/RISHEH-Industry-Solutions-Corporate-Profile#selected-work--five-different-systems)

## The business challenge

School leaders, educators, families, and students work across different daily journeys. Fragmented administrative and academic systems make it difficult to provide a coherent, role-appropriate experience.

## Product and engineering direction

Hamyar School explores a unified, configurable system for multiple schools. The documented direction brings together school operations, people and roles, class and academic workflows, family-facing experiences, and analytics. Instead of maintaining a separate codebase per school, school-specific variation is modeled through tenant configuration and experience layers.

## Services illustrated

- **Product strategy and domain architecture:** Mapping principal, school administration, teacher, student, and parent journeys into distinct responsibilities.
- **Web app and UX/UI design:** Different role-based experiences with a mobile-friendly Persian interface.
- **Data and security architecture:** Multi-tenant boundaries, permissions, and audit-aware workflows.
- **Systems integration:** Connecting education and operational modules rather than treating them as isolated screens.

## Distinctive design decision

**One platform, different school experiences.** A tenant-specific elementary experience is an extension of a common operating model, not a second standalone product.

## Evidence and current disclosure

The project's high-level scope and engineering direction are described in RISHEH's public corporate portfolio. The full engineering repository is private; this case intentionally provides no private code, school records, identities, performance metrics, or deployment details.

**Outcome we can responsibly state:** A documented multi-role, multi-tenant school platform direction with domain-oriented engineering work. School-level improvement percentages and independent production verification are not claimed.
