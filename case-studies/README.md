## How to read a case

Each study separates **challenge**, **work undertaken or documented design**, **engineering decisions**, and **evidence**. A documented capability is not automatically a verified live deployment. We intentionally omit private infrastructure, client data, fabricated KPIs, testimonials and unapproved screenshots.

**Client work vs. RISHEH-built products:** These stories include internal platforms and product initiatives. Do not interpret every entry as a commissioned external client engagement or an independently measured commercial result.
